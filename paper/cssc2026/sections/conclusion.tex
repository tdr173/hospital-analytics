\section{Conclusion and Future Work}\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented HospitAI, a layered, mostly unsupervised pipeline that turns raw
multi-table EHR data into clinician-readable insight. Raw clinical events are
reduced to one mixed-type feature vector per patient, grouped with K-Prototypes,
and mined for frequent clinical trajectories with PrefixSpan applied both
globally and \emph{per cluster}; a transversal LLM layer summarizes clusters,
interprets trajectories and answers natural-language questions through a
validate-then-execute text-to-SQL agent. On a \NPATIENTS{}-patient synthetic
cohort the pipeline yields interpretable clusters and cluster-specific
trajectories, and the LLM layer makes these results consumable without
data-analysis training. The contribution is the \emph{integration}: an
end-to-end, interpretation-first analytics flow, and the dual-scope sequence
mining that distinguishes group-specific care progressions from population-wide
ones.

Future work includes validation on de-identified real EHRs with clinician
review, incremental/online recomputation of clusters and patterns as new data
arrives, principled selection of $k$ and of the categorical weight $\gamma$, and
a tighter, formally specified safety layer around the text-to-SQL agent.
