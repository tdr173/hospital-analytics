\section{Front-end Application}

The front-end is a single Next.js~16 application that consumes the public API of every back-end service through the Traefik gateway. The App Router splits pages into a public group --- \texttt{/login}, \texttt{/forgot-password}, \texttt{/set-password} --- and an authenticated group covering \texttt{/home} (the LLM dashboard summary), \texttt{/patients}, \texttt{/encounters}, \texttt{/providers}, \texttt{/clusters} (with sub-pages for categorical features, numerical features, PCA and the LLM summary cards), \texttt{/sequences}, \texttt{/statistics}, \texttt{/ask} (the SQL agent) and \texttt{/register-user}.

Page visibility is centralised in \texttt{utils/rolePermissions.ts}: a \texttt{ROUTE\_ROLES} array maps URL prefixes to allowed roles, and \texttt{isRouteAllowedForRole(pathname, role)} resolves the longest prefix match. The function is called from the authenticated layout and from the sidebar, so hidden routes do not appear in the navigation, and a hand-typed URL is intercepted and redirected to \texttt{/home}. \texttt{/statistics}, \texttt{/clusters} and \texttt{/sequences} are manager-only; \texttt{/patients}, \texttt{/providers}, \texttt{/ask} and \texttt{/register-user} are open to managers and doctors; \texttt{/encounters} is open to all three roles. The front-end gate is convenience: every back-end endpoint re-enforces the same restriction through the \texttt{Scope} dependency of Section~\ref{subsec:authz}.
