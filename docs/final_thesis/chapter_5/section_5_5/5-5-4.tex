\subsection{Personal and Practice Dashboard Summaries}

The home page renders a single LLM-generated paragraph whose content depends on the role of the signed-in user. The \emph{personal summary} is a friendly, plain-language paragraph written in the second person, focused on the patient's active conditions, current medications, last five encounters and one gentle vitals trend; population comparisons, alarming language and invented diagnoses are explicitly forbidden. The \emph{practice summary} is a concise clinical briefing for a doctor about their own panel: panel size, recent encounter volume, top two or three conditions, and any cluster-flagged outlier worth flagging. Both are single-shot template prompts called from \texttt{/dashboard/personal-summary} and \texttt{/dashboard/practice-summary}; there is no retry, no validation, no chain of nodes --- the summaries are non-actionable text and a slightly off-topic paragraph is acceptable. This is intentionally a much simpler integration than the SQL agent of Section~\ref{subsec:sql-agent}.
