============================================================
K-PROTOTYPES CLUSTERING - FEATURE REFERENCE
============================================================

Generated by build_patient_features() in k_prototypes.py.
All features are built from SQL aggregates (lines 1-576 of
that file). Percentages next to categorical features are
population prevalences pulled from the SQL query comments.

NaN policy:
  - Sparse observation numerics (avg_bmi, avg_systolic_bp,
    avg_glucose) are NEVER mean-imputed - they are bucketed
    into categorical tiers with an explicit "Unknown" bin.
  - Count-based numerics use fillna(0) (0 = semantically "none").
  - Categorical NaNs from LEFT JOINs fall into "None" or
    "Unknown" buckets.

Log1p is applied to the three asymmetric numerics:
  total_medications, lifetime_medication_days,
  total_encounter_duration_hours.

============================================================
NUMERICAL FEATURES
============================================================

- age
    Patient age in years (from `patient`).

- total_conditions
    Total number of coded conditions ever recorded for the
    patient.

- total_procedures
    Lifetime procedure count.

- total_medications  [log1p]
    Lifetime medication count. Right-skewed → log1p to tame
    heavy polypharmacy tails.

- active_conditions_count
    Count of conditions currently active (no resolution date).

- distinct_providers_seen
    Number of distinct provider organizations the patient has
    encountered.

- lifetime_medication_days  [log1p]
    Total number of days the patient has spent on medication
    across their history. Right-skewed → log1p.

- total_encounter_duration_hours  [log1p]
    Sum of encounter durations (hours). Right-skewed → log1p.

- qaly
    Quality-Adjusted Life Years (Synthea output).

- daly
    Disability-Adjusted Life Years (Synthea output).

- inpatient_count, outpatient_count, emergency_count,
  urgentcare_count, wellness_count, ambulatory_count,
  snf_count, hospice_count
    Per-class encounter counts. fillna(0), no log1p.

--- Derived numerical -----------------------------------------

- routine_care_count
    = ambulatory_count + outpatient_count.

- acute_care_count
    = emergency_count + urgentcare_count.

- severe_care_count
    = inpatient_count + snf_count + hospice_count.

- preventive_to_reactive_ratio
    = wellness_count / (acute_care_count + severe_care_count + 1).
    Higher = more preventive orientation.

- clinical_severity_score
    PCA (1 component) over log1p([total_procedures,
    total_medications, active_conditions_count]). One-axis
    summary of overall clinical burden.
