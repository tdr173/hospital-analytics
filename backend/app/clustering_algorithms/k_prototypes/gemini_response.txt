[-1] | Complex Outliers | Extremely elderly patients with severe chronic conditions and exceptionally high use of all healthcare services. | Renal dialysis and frequent emergencies are key cost drivers. | Assign to intensive case management for immediate clinical review.
[0] | At-Risk Diabetics | Middle-aged patients managing multiple chronic conditions, primarily diabetes, with moderate emergency visit rates. | Diabetes and related complications are the main drivers of care needs. | Enroll in a diabetes management program to reduce hospitalizations.
[1] | Healthy Adults | The largest and youngest group, characterized by low healthcare utilization and minimal chronic conditions. | This population primarily seeks routine preventative care and treatment for minor acute illnesses. | Focus on digital engagement and wellness education to maintain health.
[2] | Maternity & Women's Health | Younger female patients with a high volume of procedures, primarily related to pregnancy and obstetric care. | Healthcare interactions are frequent but focused on routine maternity monitoring. | Offer specialized maternity care coordination and support services.
[3] | Well-Managed Seniors | Older patients with high rates of hypertension and diabetes who are actively engaged in preventative care. | Despite a high medication burden, proactive wellness visits result in very low emergency use. | Support medication adherence and continue frequent preventative care appointments.
